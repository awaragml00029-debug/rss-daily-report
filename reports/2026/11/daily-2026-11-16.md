Much effort has been devoted to predicting the epitope specificity of TCRs, but bioin-formatic methods for inferring HLA restriction from TCR data remain limited in their ac-curacy and applicability. Here, we developed a method to identify HLA restriction of public TCRs (TCR-RATE), based on detection of increased Relative Frequencies (RF) of antigen-specific public clonotypes occurring in individuals carrying a particular HLA allele. The method was developed utilizing a training set including 264,229 clonotypes associated with 475,300 reads from SARS-CoV-2 spike-specific TCRs from 55 differ-ent HLA-typed donors, expressed in CD4 T cells. Of these TCRs, 176 (5,281 reads) were recognized in 5 or more donors. HLA restriction could be inferred for 74.4% of the public clonotypes and 71.9% of the reads (p=0.01 Fisher's exact test). These re-strictions were further validated in two independent cohorts of spike-reactive cells from 27 and 60 individuals, with increased RFs (≥3) observed for 73.5 % and 78.5% of TCRs with inferred restrictions in the two validation sets, respectively. We created a web-based interface for TCR-RATE to make the method available to the research community. This tool has broad applicability, as TCR-HLA associations can be used to select clonotypes for further experimental validation of TCR specificity.
